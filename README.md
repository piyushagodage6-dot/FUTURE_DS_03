# Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis (Task 3)

## Objective
Analyze customer behavior data to understand how users move through the funnel
from visitors to interested users and final customers.

## Dataset
E-commerce Customer Behavior Dataset (Kaggle - Mini version)

## Funnel Stages
- Visitors: Total unique customers
- Interested Users: Customers who purchased at least one item
- Customers: Customers with total spend greater than zero

## Tools Used
- Power BI

## Key Insights
- A major drop-off is observed at the final conversion stage.
- Many users show interest but do not convert into paying customers.
- Discount and membership strategies influence purchasing behavior.

## Recommendations
- Improve customer engagement strategies
- Offer targeted discounts
- Strengthen loyalty programs
- Retarget interested but inactive users

## Deliverables
- Power BI funnel dashboard
- Conversion rate analysis
- Business recommendations
